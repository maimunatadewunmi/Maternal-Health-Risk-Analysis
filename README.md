# Maternal-Health-Risk-Analysis

## Overview
Pregnancy can become dangerous when certain health conditions are present.

In this project, I analyzed the Maternal Health Risk dataset from Kaggle to explore how factors like: 
- Age
- Blood sugar levels
- Blood pressure levels
affect pregnancy risk.

The goal was not just to analyze data, but to clearly communicate using Data storytelling principles.

## Tools Used
- SQL
- PowerBI

## Key Insights
- Pregnancy risk increased with Age, with most high risk cases occurring among women aged 35 and above.
- Blood sugar levels showed a strong relationship with pregnancy risk
- Women with Hypertension Stage 2 had higher chances of complications.
- The strongest finding came from combining both conditions: 80% of women with both diabetes and hypertension stage 2 were classified as high-risk pregnancies.

## Lesson Learnt
- Good analysis starts with asking focused questions.
- Insight with context is just a report.
- Data storytelling is just as important as technical skills.

## Disclaimer
This dataset was sourced from Kaggle and may not fully represent real-world trends.
